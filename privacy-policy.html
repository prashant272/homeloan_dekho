Introduction

Welcome to AAds Financial Private Limited (“we,” “us,” “our”). We are a loan facilitator providing Business Loans, Home Loans, Home Loans, and Car Loans through our collaboration with various private and NBFC banks. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website or use our mobile application (collectively, the “Site”). By using our Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Important Disclaimers

AADS Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. is not a lender. We act only as a loan facilitator connecting applicants with RBI-registered Banks and NBFCs. All loan approvals, interest rates, documentation requirements, and loan terms are solely decided by the respective lending institutions.

RBI Guidelines: All loans are processed only through RBI-registered Banks and NBFCs. We do not influence the decision of any lender.

Data Safety Disclaimer: We do not guarantee loan approval or influence the lender’s internal underwriting, eligibility checks, or approval decisions. Your data is shared only with authorized lending partners for the purpose of processing your application.

Partner List Transparency: We work with multiple Banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions. A complete updated list of our lending partners is available on our “Partners” page.
